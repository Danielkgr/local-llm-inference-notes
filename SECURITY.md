# Security Policy

Local LLM Inference Notes is a record of measurements rather than a service,
and its accompanying tools are small and standalone.  This policy covers the
notes, the tools, and the code they ship.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems to Daniel Glynn-Roe at
daniel.glynnroe@gmail.com.  Use email rather than a public issue so the details
can be reviewed before disclosure.

Please include what happened, the steps to reproduce it, and the version or
commit you were using.  If your report contains a proof of concept that could
spread on its own, describe what it does without attaching it, and share the
working copy once we confirm we understand it.

## What to expect

We reply within 14 days.  We confirm we have received your report, and we tell
you when we think the problem is fixed.  We name you in the fix notes unless
you ask to stay anonymous, and we never ask you to sign a non-disclosure
agreement to report a problem here.  This project does not offer a bug bounty.

## Public disclosure

We will not publish the details of a reported problem until it is fixed, and we
ask that anyone who reports one keep it private until then.  Once a fix is
released we disclose what was wrong and who reported it, unless the reporter
prefers otherwise.
